SUNSET BAY HEIST

An open-world crime game · Sunset Bay

You are Rook Marlowe, a getaway driver with one last debt to pay. The Marlowes want their money back, the Bayview crew wants you gone, and the Sunset Bay police want you in a cell. Steal cars, outrun the law, run five jobs across the city — and pull off the big score at the Sunset Bank before the sun goes down.

W A S D — walk / drive
Mouse drag — look around
Shift — run
E — steal / enter / exit a car
Space — handbrake
F — fire pistol   R — reload
Esc / P — pause   M — mute

An original game. Sunset Bay, its people and its cars are invented — nothing here is from any commercial game.
